# safari-plus

Every rustle in the grass is now a Safari Zone heartbeat. Every wild
encounter — and every fated, scripted, one-of-a-kind meeting — opens
the native Emerald Safari scene instead of a normal fight. No more
punching the thing you came to catch. You throw, it stares back, the
ball rocks... and Hoenn holds its breath. Trainer battles are
untouched: humans still get the full beatdown they deserve.

This is the whole game, re-tuned around the thrill of the catch.
Your skills grow because you throw balls, not because you bought
something. Your starter becomes a legend. Your duplicates become
fuel. Welcome to the hunt.

## The four safari skills

Forget the shop. There is no shop. Four lines level up purely from
things you do in the field, and the Start menu's SAFARI row shows
every level, every progress bar, every current bonus. MAX a skill and
you feel like the warden of the whole Zone.

- **Safari Up (catch, max 60)** — each level adds +0.35 catch rate,
  all the way to +21.0. You earn it by catching: harder species pay
  more, and your first legendary of a line pays a fortune. Toss
  well, and the Zone starts to feel rigged in your favor. Because
  it is.
- **Likeable (max 30)** — wild mons just... stop running. The flee
  divisor climbs toward 4x with every level, fed by tense
  stare-downs (+6 every time one watches carefully and stays) and by
  every Pokéblock you lob (+8). Get to know them. Feed them. They'll
  stick around.
- **Ball Recovery (max 70)** — your stock refills faster and faster,
  from one ball every 12 seconds down to one every 5. Every step in
  the field, every overworld throw, every finished encounter feeds
  it. You will never again stare at an empty pocket while a shiny
  blinks at you. Probably.
- **Ball Cap (max 30)** — from 10 balls up to 70. Clean throwing
  pays: first-ball catches and fresh dex entries earn the most, and
  every wasted ball shaves a little off. Throw like a surgeon, carry
  like a quartermaster.

## Overworld mode: tap it, throw at it

Turn on `OVERWORLD` (needs Untamed-Advanced or Wilds of Hoenn with
visible encounters) and the hunt leaves the battle scene entirely.
Wild mons wander the grass in front of you — tap one and your ball
arcs through the air at it, GO-style. No battle, no menu, just you,
one second of flight, and the rock of the ball.

The foe is the tapped actor itself: its species, level, personality,
moves, shininess. A catch despawns it through the spawner's own
removal; a break-out leaves it wandering, unimpressed. Only real
spawns are tappable — scripted set-pieces stay battle-only.

- **Your equipped ball is the one you throw.** Tap the ball chip to
  open the bag at Poké Balls and USE one to equip it (safari balls
  stay home). The HUD shows the equipped icon and live count, the
  thrown art matches, and the stored catch remembers which ball did
  the job. Empty pockets throw nothing — the mon slips away.
- **Every ball rolls its own fate.** Safari math stays in the Zone;
  out here each ball type plays by its own odds — Greats beat Pokés,
  Ultras beat Greats, Masters never miss, and Net, Dive, Nest,
  Repeat and Timer balls all honor their engine conditionals. Get
  close: throws from one tile away catch far better than long bombs.
  Hungry ball? The Oldale Town mart is vanilla stock — come prepared.
- **Berries are your aim assist.** Four HUD buttons under the ball
  chip: Razz (1.5x catch), Pinap (1.2x catch, double candy),
  Golden Razz (2.5x catch), Silver Pinap (1.8x catch, triple candy).
  One at a time, spent from the bag, eaten by the next throw —
  catch or fail. Event berries refuse to be planted: the soil itself
  rejects them.
- **No prompts, no paperwork.** New species and special duplicates
  are kept; ordinary dupes auto-convert to candy in the caught size
  quantity. Party EXP, EVs, level-up moves and evolutions-waiting
  all resolve through HUD toasts with the success chime and the
  species' cry instead of the battle jingle. Skill level-ups toast
  right there on the grass.
- **The world feeds you.** Glowing item balls dot the ground — 162
  of them: free balls, berries, evolution stones and trinkets, even
  the occasional mystery egg, unlocking as your player level rises.
  They recharge in 5 minutes, and every pickup pays trainer XP.

## Candies: duplicates are fuel

Every dupe you release into the machine comes back as power. All of
it lives in one CANDY POUCH (a single ITEMS row, so your bag never
drowns) — USE the pouch to browse your stash, pick a candy, pick a
mon. Old saves migrate in on load.

- **EXP Candy** (XS 100 / S 800 / M 3000 / L 10000 / XL 30000 XP by
  tier, with juicy bonuses for pseudo-legendaries and legendaries)
  feeds straight through the native award — messages, bars,
  level-ups, move learning, evolution. Turn the toggle off and the
  exchange becomes a clean release instead.
- **Stat Candy** drops on every single catch in the species' own EV
  yield — the fast mon's speed, the tank's defense, exactly what it
  would have taught you in a fight. Each catch rolls the mon's size
  (most are normal, a rare giant bursts with triple candy, and the
  odd runt carries nothing at all). USE them inside their window —
  S below 100 EVs, L from 100 to 200, XL up to 252 — and sculpt any
  mon to the cap, 252 a stat, 510 total... or 1512 with the toggle
  that breaks the old gods' limit.
- **Species Candy** is the alchemist's dream: converting a duplicate
  drops candy named for its whole line, tinted the species' own
  color, and feeding it raises every tied-lowest IV by +1 — a
  0-attack runt and a 30-speed marvel both answered by the same
  sweet logic. Perfect 31s cost a premium. Off-line mons, eggs and
  maxed specimens are politely refused.
- **Release from the PC pays too**, same rates, same toggles. Eggs
  and your partner pay nothing — some things aren't for sale.

## Partner: your starter becomes a myth

The Torchic, Treecko and Mudkip lines carry boosted blood: +10 HP,
+25 Attack, +10 Defense, +25 Sp. Atk, +10 Sp. Def, +30 Speed, folded
straight into the native stat math, natures honored. Your first
gifted one bonds as your partner — it keeps the boost through every
evolution, it cannot be released (the menu simply won't offer), and
it answers to a new ability: Intimidate, Lightning Rod, or Mud Force
(+30% Attack in rain). The rival's matching mons hit back just as
hard. Everything else in Hoenn stays exactly as Arceus made it.

And then there's Mom. Talk to her in Littleroot and she'll tutor your
partner twelve exclusive moves — four per line, unlocked at 1 / 3 /
5 / 6 badges, paid in species candy — friendship-powered finishers,
priority trickery, traps, shields and storms, all riding the native
effects engine. The rival fields badge-legal moves of his own, and
Wally shows up with the third line's base form at the back of his
party, right where it hurts most.

## Player level: you are training too

With `PLAYER LEVEL` on, you're on the GO 1–80 curve and everything
pays: every safari skill tick feeds you trainer XP (half rations
until level 40 — the early road is deliberately steep), catches pay
400 (1000 for a new species, 1500 for a legend), wins, gyms, rivals,
Elite Four, evolutions, hatches and raid dens all chip in. Level-ups
toast over the grass and line up in the SAFARI row. This is the
long game, trainer — eighty levels of becoming the person the
strongest Pokémon want to be caught by.

## Options

- `SAFARI` (default ON) — the whole Zone treatment above.
- `OVERWORLD` (default OFF) — tap-to-throw GO-style catching.
- `EXP YIELD` (default 50%) — 0/10/25/50/75/100/150/200/300/400/500%.
  At 0%, catches pay no EXP and grant no EVs (candy still flows).
- `SPEED UP` (default ON) — safari throw/catch animation at ~3x,
  arc and all. Pure vanilla at OFF.
- `MASTERY` (default ON) — per-species catch counters persist
  across sessions; mastered lines roll guaranteed plus bonus 31 IVs
  (5 catches: 0+1 ... 300 catches: 3+3).
- `IV GUARD` (default 3) — duplicates with at least this many
  perfect-31 stats ask to be kept before any candy question.
- `1512 EVS` (default OFF) — 1512 total EVs instead of 510 (252 per
  stat stays); covers safari, trainer battles with the trainer mod,
  and candy use.
- `STAT CANDY` / `SPECIES CANDY` / `EXP CANDY` (default ON) — each
  candy flow on its own switch.
- `PARTNER` (default ON) — boosted starter lines, release guard,
  Mom's tutoring, rival/Wally rewrites.
- `PLAYER LEVEL` (default ON) — the GO 1–80 trainer curve.

## Layout

- `main.lua` — entry: options, boots everything.
- `shared.lua` — upgrade counts/prices, stock, wall-clock regen.
- `battle/safari_force.lua` — wild/fated encounters into the Safari
- scene (tutorials and trainer parties excluded); stock write-back;
- regen freeze/thaw around battles.
- `battle/catch_exp.lua` — safari catches pay party-wide EXP through
- the normal in-scene award presentation.
- `battle/no_balls_run.lua` — running out of balls plays the run-away
- pipeline (got-away message + flee sound, no zone eject) instead of
- the safari out-of-balls pipeline.
- `battle/factors.lua` — catch/escape factor bonuses; battle stock
- from the live stock.
- `battle/ball_guard.lua` — zero-ball battle end; live stock sync.
- `battle/pokeblock_case.lua` — POKéBLOCK CASE gate on flag 0x5F.
- `battle/duplicate_candy.lua` — duplicate catches convert to EXP CANDY.
- `battle/iv_guard.lua` — IV GUARD setting: high perfect-IV duplicates
- ask to keep before any candy question.
- `battle/catch_speed.lua` — SPEED UP toggle substeps the catch animation.
- `battle/mastery.lua` — MASTERY toggle: per-species catch counters with
- wild-encounter IV bonuses (plus `SP.masteryCount`/`SP.masteryTierFor`
- exports so overworld mon gen applies the same tiers).
- `battle/safari_xp.lua` — per-skill XP store; catch/like/
- regen/cap awards from catches, flee-fails, POKéBLOCKs,
- steps, overworld throws/catches, encounter ends, and new species.
- `battle/partner.lua` — PARTNER toggle: boosted starter-line base
- stats for the first gifted partner and the rival, release guard.
- `data/partner_moves.lua` — the 12 partner-move defs (home
- ids 355-366, relocated past dex-mod moves when present),
- badge unlocks, and candy costs.
- `battle/partner_moves.lua` — move install, tutoring, and the
- rival/Wally party rewrite.
- `overworld/bag_balls.lua` — permanent virtual SAFARI BALL bag slot
- mirroring the live stock.
- `overworld/safari_menu.lua` — Start menu SAFARI row (same hook as
- RADIO NEWS, separate id): four chained message bubbles, one per
- skill, each `Safari <skill> Lv <lvl>/<max> <prog>/<need>` plus its
- current effect line.
- `overworld/entrance.lua` — the exit-door guard inside the zone
- south is parked 1 left and 6 up while on, so the way back out
- stays open; it returns when you leave or switch the mod off.
- `overworld/overworld_catch.lua` — OVERWORLD toggle: tap
- Untamed's visible mons, throw the equipped ball (never a safari
- ball) with landing prediction; the mon hides inside the ball
- through rock/still/fade (reappearing only on a break-out), and
- automatic rewards (party EXP/EVs, stat/species candy,
- EXP-candy conversion).
